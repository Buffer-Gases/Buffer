# T27 — Accessibility (Buffer 4.9.0)

**Covered:** keyboard-only use, focus visibility and order, modals, names and roles, headings, live regions, contrast (pixel and clinical skins, dark and light), colour-only cues, reduced motion, target size, reflow and zoom (320 px, 640×400 = 200% of 1280×800), and screen-reader output via Playwright aria_snapshot (page.accessibility is removed in Playwright 1.63, so I used locator.aria_snapshot plus my own DOM scripts). Views covered: Today, Round builder, Learn, lesson ph and hh with questions, Labs, all 10 tools, all 15 models, Practice, all 11 session modes, the 4 speed drills, the case library, chat ch-dka, image im-capno, sim sim-dka, Progress, Settings, Glossary, the gas log, the save sheet and the OCR confirm sheet. Scripts and raw JSON: /tmp/claude-0/-home-claude/80312b5f-0e45-5589-b131-fcf824f33899/scratchpad/beta/t27/. Screenshots: shots/T27-*.png.

**Verdict: 5/10.** The foundations are good: native buttons almost everywhere, labelled inputs and selects, aria-pressed on most toggles, aria-expanded on modules, a global 3 px :focus-visible ring, honest reduced-motion support (every model and confetti stop under prefers-reduced-motion; rAF counts were 0), a dark theme that passes AA for text, toasts in a polite live region, and a speed drill that announces feedback and moves focus to Next. The dynamic parts let keyboard and screen-reader users down. Almost every answer, toggle and timer tick rebuilds innerHTML and drops focus to <body>. Sessions, lessons and cases are silent to screen readers. The four sheets are not real modals. The timed sim steals focus every 3 s. Hold-only menus hide actions from the keyboard. Right and wrong are shown by red and green alone in speed drills and sims. The light theme misses AA on its accent and muted text, and form borders are about 1.5:1.

Counts: critical 1, major 16, minor 22, polish 2, idea 7

## Critical

### T27-1 · ui8.js · Timed sim: SIMT interval -> simAdvance + simRender (ui8.js ~l.91-105)
- **What:** The sim re-renders the whole #v-cases view with innerHTML every 3 s (simPace 3). Each re-render destroys the focused button, so keyboard focus drops to <body> every 3 s, and a screen reader loses its reading position. A keyboard-only or screen-reader user cannot reach or press the action buttons. The sim is unusable without a mouse or touch.
- **Evidence:** Playwright 1280x800: openCase("sim-dka"), focus the "Venous gas" button. Focus stays on it at 1 s and 2 s, then document.activeElement = BODY at 3, 4 and 5 s. The next Tab lands on "← Cases" (top of the page).
- **Fix:** Render the sim skeleton once. On each tick, update only the clock text, the vitals (.vits), the severity bar, Results and Events in place, keyed by id. Leave the action buttons in the DOM, and only toggle .done/disabled on them. If a full re-render is kept, save document.activeElement (data-act key) before and re-focus the same action after. Put the Events list in role="log" aria-live="polite" (announce only the newest event) and the Results in aria-live="polite".

## Major

### T27-2 · ui5.js · openRoundBuilder() draw(): checkbox onchange and preset [data-p] onclick
- **What:** Toggling a leg checkbox with Space, or pressing a preset (Quick 10 / Standard 25 / Everything) with Enter, calls draw(), which rewrites sc.innerHTML. Focus is lost to <body>, so a keyboard user must Tab from the top of the page after every tick. The builder is also not a usable modal (see the next finding).
- **Evidence:** kb3.py: focus .sheet input[type=checkbox], press Space -> activeElement BODY. Focus [data-p="10"], press Enter -> BODY.
- **Fix:** Do not rebuild the sheet on change. Update only the checkbox states and the "Start · N min" label and disabled state of #rbBuild in place. Or, if draw() stays, re-focus the element with the same data-i or data-p after draw().

### T27-3 · ui5.js · openRoundBuilder() sheet
- **What:** Round builder dialog: focus is not moved into it (it stays on #tRound behind the scrim), and Tab walks out to the page behind it. Escape does not close it. It has no aria-modal, and its title is a <b>, not a heading. Cancel or Build removes the sheet without returning focus to #tRound, so focus falls to <body>.
- **Evidence:** kb.py: Enter on #tRound -> focus still on #tRound and the next Tabs go to "The Round", "Real printouts"… behind the scrim. Escape -> the sheet is still open. Enter on #rbCancel -> activeElement BODY.
- **Fix:** Use the shared modal helper (see the ui1.js finding): role="dialog" aria-modal="true" aria-labelledby pointing at an <h2>; on open, focus the first preset button or the heading (tabindex=-1); trap Tab and Shift+Tab inside .sheet; close on Escape; on close, restore focus to the opener (#tRound, or the round-bar button that opened it); set inert on <main>, the header and #roundbar while it is open.

### T27-4 · ui6.js · saveSheet()
- **What:** Save-to-log sheet: it focuses #lgTag (good), but Tab past Cancel leaves the dialog (aria-modal="true" is declared, but the page behind it is not inert). Escape does not close it. After Save or Cancel, focus goes to <body> instead of back to #iSave.
- **Evidence:** kb3.py: Tab from #lgNo -> BODY, then "Buffer, today", "Today"… Escape -> the sheet is still open. Enter on Cancel -> activeElement BODY.
- **Fix:** Use the shared modal helper: a focus trap, an Escape that closes it, inert on the background, and focus returned to #iSave (or the button that opened it) on close.

### T27-5 · ui10.js · ocrSheet()
- **What:** Photo-confirm sheet: focus is not moved into it. activeElement stays BODY or on the file label, so a screen reader is not told that "Check the numbers" opened. There is no focus trap and no Escape, and focus is not returned to #pPhoto on close.
- **Evidence:** kb3.py: after ocrSheet(...), activeElement = BODY. Escape -> still open. Tab from #ocNo -> BODY, then the header behind it.

### T27-6 · ui1.js · sheet() (hand-kit action sheet)
- **What:** The generic action sheet closes on Escape, but it does not move focus into the sheet. It has no aria-modal, no trap and no focus return. Escape uses stopPropagation, which does not stop the other document-level keydown listeners (the session listener would also see the Escape). There is no shared modal helper, so each of the four sheets in the app handles keyboard differently.
- **Evidence:** kb3.py: sheet("Test",...) -> focus stays on the lesson button behind the sheet. aria-modal = null.
- **Fix:** Add one helper, openModal(sc,{opener,initial}), in ui1.js. It should set role="dialog" aria-modal="true"; focus the initial element; trap Tab; close on Escape (use stopImmediatePropagation, and register with capture:true); set inert on main, header and #roundbar; and on close remove inert and opener.focus(). Use it in sheet(), openRoundBuilder(), saveSheet() and ocrSheet().

### T27-7 · ui2.js · drawSession() / answerQ / answerGym / answerFit; caseFb
- **What:** In every session mode, answering re-renders #v-session with innerHTML. Focus drops to <body>, and nothing announces the result: there is no aria-live region in the session, and #toasts is the only one. A screen-reader user presses an option and hears nothing, then has to search the page for the next question.
- **Evidence:** kb2.py: startSession("steps"), focus the first .opt, press Enter -> activeElement BODY. Live regions after the answer: only #toasts (empty). The feedback "Answer: Alkalaemia. pH 7.52…" is in the DOM but not announced. Also, startSession leaves focus on BODY.
- **Fix:** Add one visually hidden <div id="sLive" role="status" aria-live="polite"> outside #v-session, and write the outcome into it ("Wrong. Answer: Alkalaemia. pH 7.52, normal 7.35–7.45"). After each redraw, move focus to the next unanswered question's h3 (give it tabindex=-1), or to #fbNext when the case is complete. On session start, focus the first question heading.

### T27-8 · ui1.js · renderLesson(): lesson questions (#lQ .qitem .opt)
- **What:** Answering a lesson question disables all of its options, including the focused one, so focus drops to <body>. The .why feedback is unhidden but not announced. Right and wrong are shown only by red and green colour on the disabled buttons, whose kbd letters stay A–D.

### T27-9 · ui8.js · Chat and image cases: case step answer (#v-cases .opt)
- **What:** After an answer in a chat or image case, focus is lost (the focused button is disabled or re-rendered). The "What happens" and "Right." feedback is not announced. New chat bubbles are added to .chat, which is not a log or live region.
- **Evidence:** chat.py: openCase("ch-dka"), Enter on option 1 -> activeElement BODY. .chat has no role or aria-live.

### T27-10 · ui1.js · go(): view changes
- **What:** Changing view never moves focus or announces anything. The <title> stays "Buffer Blood Gas Trainer" on every view. When the activating button is replaced (Labs tiles, Today tiles, Practice tiles, back crumbs), focus falls to <body>, and the next Tab restarts at the brand button, which is 7 stops before the content. Screen-reader users are not told that the page changed.
- **Evidence:** kb.py: Enter on the Learn tab -> focus stays on the tab, and nothing is announced. Enter on the Practice "Step by step" tile -> BODY.
- **Fix:** At the end of go(): set document.title = "<view title> · Buffer"; focus the view's h1 (h1.tabIndex=-1; h1.focus({preventScroll:true})), or the first heading if there is no h1, unless the view was opened by a bottom-tab press (then keep focus on the tab and announce the view name in a polite live region).

### T27-11 · kit.js · Hand kit: hold menus (MENUS) and hold-to-explain hints
- **What:** Several actions exist only behind a long-press with a pointer: "Mark as done" and "Mark as not done" (lessons), "Reset its review schedule" (mastery tiles), "Copy link" (tools), "Forget my result" (shifts), "Show in the concept web", and "Teach it back" from a lesson. Shift+F10 and the ContextMenu key do nothing (the contextmenu default is prevented). The hint text (data-hint on heat squares, pips, XP, streak) is shown only in a pointer tooltip with role=tooltip, which is not referenced by aria-describedby. Fails WCAG 2.1.1.
- **Evidence:** kb3.py: focus .llist button[data-l], press Shift+F10 or ContextMenu -> no sheet. grep shows "Mark as done", "Reset its review", "Forget my result" and "Copy link" exist only in kit.js.
- **Fix:** Open the same MENUS sheet from the keyboard: on a "contextmenu" event (which fires for Shift+F10 and the Menu key) whose target matches a MENUS selector, call sheet(...) instead of preventDefault only. Add a visible "⋯" more button (aria-label "More actions for <name>", aria-haspopup="dialog") to lesson rows, mastery tiles and tool tiles. For data-hint items, expose the text with aria-describedby or a visually hidden span, and show the bubble on focus as well as on hold.

### T27-16 · shell.html · .sheet{touch-action:none} (line 468)
- **What:** touch-action:none on .sheet blocks finger scrolling inside every sheet. When a sheet is taller than 86vh (the OCR confirm sheet with 13 fields, the Round builder with 6 or more legs, or any sheet at large text sizes or 200% zoom), the top of the sheet cannot be reached by touch. In the OCR sheet that top part holds the heading, the privacy note, the Arterial/Venous toggle and the pH field.
