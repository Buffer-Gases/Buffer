# D29 · timed simulations (ui8.js sim engine + c12.js sim-* objects)

## What changed (summary)
The single "severity" lerp between two fixed anchors was replaced by a hidden physiology state per sim (c12.js `x0`, `step()`, `out()`), with timed treatment effects (onset and duration), per-variable lab drift, titrated oxygen through the alveolar gas equation (FiO2, PaO2 and SpO2 now move with oxygen therapy), random variants, harms and not-needed actions, a read-the-gas question on the gases the sim makes, tiered endings that use the target times, a score, and a richer debrief. The view is built once; ticks update only the parts that change. The running sim is saved in sessionStorage.

Checked with a headless harness (scratchpad d29/strat.py): for all 5 sims and all variants, an optimal plan ends in the win ending; optimal care followed by up to 10 h of waiting stays stable (no arrest; DKA arrests only when fluids stop after one litre, with a nurse prompt); doing nothing arrests (DKA 163, sepsis 97, asthma 44, hyperK 38, vent 46 min); clicking every action in list order or in reverse never reaches the clean ending (harm, detours, late, incomplete or arrest). The vitals at T+0 equal the vignette in every sim. Browser checks at 390×844 and 1280×800: no page errors, no horizontal scroll, focus stays on the focused action across ticks, the question takes focus and keys 1–4 and Enter work, reload and Back/Forward resume, the Practice resume card works, and the hand-over confirm works.
