# D30 · German glossary (ui9.js: GLOSS, glossParts, sayDE, RENDER.gloss, Learn tile, Yuzu vocab pack)

All edits are in the glossary half of /home/claude/buffer/ui9.js (from `/* German glossary */` to the end). The links half (D20) is untouched. The build passes and node tests/run.js gives 147/0. Checked in headless Chromium at 390×844 and 1280×800: no page errors (only the offline font request fails) and no horizontal scroll. Screenshots are in scratchpad/d30/.

Data model: GLOSS is now a list of objects made by gl9(id,en,de,pl,cat,ex,{abbr,alt,reg,pp,ex2,noAbk}). It grew from 76 to 144 rows. Every row has an example, every noun has a plural or '–' (no plural), and every verb has its Perfekt. Sources for the German: Duden (articles, plurals), DGP S2k guideline on prolonged weaning (AWMF 020-015; "Weaning"), the GRC/ERC German guidelines 2021 ("Kreislaufstillstand", ROSC), AWMF 020-004 on NIV (Partial-/Globalinsuffizienz, hypoxämisch/hyperkapnisch), Sepsis-3 (Singer, JAMA 2016;315:801), Berlin ARDS definition (Horovitz 100–200 = moderate), PROSEVA (prone ≥16 h).
